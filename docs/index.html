Insights & recommendations

Every number below is computed from the data by scripts/export_dashboard_data.py.

About the data. All data is synthetic, generated with a fixed random seed and modeled on the operations of a small multi-channel food business. It contains no real customers, sales or events.

Definitions. Revenue = item sales minus discounts (tips excluded). Gross profit = revenue − food cost. Repeat customer rate = share of tracked customers (those with a customer ID) who placed 2+ orders in the selection.
